# Reports hub

Screen: **Reports** (`/reports`). API: `GET /api/v1/report-hub/catalog`, `/run/{key}?filters`, `/export/{key}?format=xlsx|pdf&filters`.
Financial statements (P&L, balance sheet, trial balance) stay on **Financial Statements** (`/reports-bi`).

Definitions live in `transport-backend/src/main/resources/reports/report-catalog.json`, generated by
`tools/reports/generate_catalog.py` and checked against the full schema with `tools/reports/test_catalog_sql.py`
(apply all Flyway migrations to a local PostgreSQL first). Every run is scoped to the user's company; branch-bound users
see only their branch. Finance reports need COMPANY_ADMIN / ADMIN / ACCOUNTANT / BRANCH_MANAGER / VIEWER. Screen shows
up to 5,000 rows; export up to 50,000. Numeric columns marked as totals get a totals row (screen, Excel, PDF).

| Category | Reports |
|---|---|
| Monthly & management | Monthly business summary (billing, collections, fuel, expenses, maintenance, payroll, margin), Management KPIs (19) |
| Operations | Trip register, Daily dispatch summary, Weighbridge shortage |
| Vehicles & trips | Vehicle performance & profitability, Document expiry (insurance/fitness/permit/licence) |
| Bookings & delivery | Booking & delivery status, Pending deliveries |
| Invoices & receipts | Sales register (GST), GST summary (rate-wise), Customer-wise revenue, Receipt register |
| Outstanding & pending | Customer outstanding & ageing, Unbilled completed trips, Pending approvals & actions |
| Drivers & settlement | Driver performance, Driver payroll register, Driver advances & balance |
| Fuel & expenses | Fuel register, Fuel efficiency by vehicle, Expense register, Expense summary by category |
| Maintenance & work orders | Work order register, Maintenance cost by vehicle, Maintenance request register, Spare parts consumption |
| Warehouse & stock | Stock valuation, Reorder list, Stock movement summary, Inventory ledger, Spare parts purchase register |
| Financial & accounting | Day book, Account ledger / cash & bank book (running balance), Account balances (period) |

Rules used across reports: revenue = taxable value of invoices not DRAFT/CANCELLED; collections = APPROVED receipts;
fuel cost = APPROVED fuel entries; expenses = APPROVED/PAID; maintenance = COMPLETED work orders' actual cost;
payroll = POSTED/PAID gross; delivered quantity = weighbridge delivered weight when recorded, else planned.
